# Project name:   MastodonHub
## Description: 
Purdue University offers a variety of clubs and events. As new students we are unaware of these activities and find it challenging to navigate the PFW portal to locate our interests and we are often left wondering about the events. We aim to create an app which enables easy Club and Events enrollment, Purdue events calendar with ability to subscribe for notifications, get to know the people involved in these clubs and events etc. 

## Academic and product value:
-  Streamlined Experience
-  Increased Participation
-  Community Building
